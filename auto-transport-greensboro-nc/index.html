Greensboro Auto Transport, Handled First Class

Greensboro sits at the crossroads of the Carolinas — where I-40 meets I-85 — and First Class Auto Transport puts that location to work. From our office on North Greene Street downtown, we arrange door-to-door car shipping across the Piedmont Triad: Greensboro, High Point, Burlington, Kernersville, Jamestown, and Summerfield.

The Triad is one of the East Coast's busiest logistics corridors, and car carriers are part of that flow. Vehicles moving between the Northeast, Atlanta, and Florida pass through Greensboro daily on I-85, which keeps pickup windows short — often 1–3 days — and pricing sharp for both regional and long-haul auto transport.

College town, too: between UNCG, NC A&T, Elon, and Guilford, we move a wave of student vehicles every August and May. Parents shipping a car for the school year should book 2–3 weeks ahead of the semester rush for the best rates and dates.
